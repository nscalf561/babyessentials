Daily Baby

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

Baby Essentials ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Daily Baby mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who this app is for

Daily Baby is intended for parents and caregivers. It is not directed at children under 13. You should only add information about a baby if you are the parent, guardian, or an invited caregiver with permission to do so.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address and password (stored and authenticated by our backend provider, Supabase).
  • Baby profile information: baby name and workspace membership (owner or caregiver).
  • Daily photos: photos you capture or choose, including date taken and optional image metadata (dimensions, file size).
  • Baby log entries: information you enter such as sleep, feedings, diapers, journal notes, weight, and related timestamps.
  • App settings: reminder preferences, optional "save to Photos app" preference, and log visibility settings.
  • Device data stored on your phone: a local copy of photos, logs, and settings so the App works offline and loads quickly.

How we use information

We use your information to:

  • Provide the App, including syncing data across your devices when signed in.
  • Let you share a baby profile with invited caregivers.
  • Send local push notifications for daily photo reminders (if you allow notifications).
  • Save a copy of photos to your device Photos app when you enable that setting.
  • Create optional slideshow videos on your device from your stored photos.
  • Maintain streaks and in-app achievements stored locally per baby profile.

Where data is stored

  • Cloud: account, settings, baby profiles, log entries, photo metadata, and photo files are stored with Supabase (hosted infrastructure). Photos are stored in private cloud storage accessible only to members of that baby profile.
  • On your device: SQLite database, photo files in app storage, and small preferences in secure storage (for example, active baby selection and sign-in session tokens).

Sharing with others

If you own a baby profile, you may invite caregivers. Caregivers you invite can view and add photos and log entries for that baby according to the app's sharing features. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage. These providers process data on our behalf to run the App. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies and our agreements with them.

Permissions

The App may request access to:

  • Camera and photo library — to capture or choose daily photos.
  • Notifications — to remind you to take a daily photo.
  • Photos app (optional) — only if you turn on "Also save to Photos app."

You can change or revoke permissions in your device settings.

Retention

We keep your information while your account is active. If you delete your account in the App, we delete your account and associated cloud data, including baby profiles you own and your contributions to shared profiles, subject to reasonable backup retention by our providers for a limited period.

Your choices

  • Update reminder and photo settings in the App.
  • Revoke caregiver access or invites from the Family section.
  • Delete your account and data in Settings → Delete account and data. If you own a baby profile, that profile and its data are deleted for all caregivers.
  • Sign out at any time from Settings.

Security

We use industry-standard measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls on cloud data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

International users

If you use the App from outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.

Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email support@babyessentials.com or visit our Support page.
