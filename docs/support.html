Support

We're here to help with Daily Baby on iOS and Android.

Contact

Email us at support@babyessentials.app. We typically respond within a few business days.

Please include the email address you use to sign in and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords.

Common topics

  • Sign in or email confirmation — check spam, then request a new confirmation or password reset from the sign-in screen.
  • Caregiver invites — open the invite link on the device where Daily Baby is installed, sign in, then accept the invite.
  • Reminders — allow notifications in iOS/Android settings and confirm your reminder times in Settings.
  • Delete account — Settings → Delete account and data. This permanently removes your account and data.
